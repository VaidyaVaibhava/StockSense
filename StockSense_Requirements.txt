------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------

--- 1.1 Authentication & User Management ---
FR-01  [Must]   Users sign up and log in using secure password hashing (bcrypt/argon2).
                AC: Passwords never stored or logged in plaintext; hash + salt verified in code review.
FR-02  [Must]   OTP-based password reset workflow via email/SMS.
                AC: OTP expires in <=10 minutes; max 5 attempts before lockout.
FR-03  [Must]   Post-authentication routing to role-based dashboard.
                AC: Warehouse Staff never see financial widgets; verified via RBAC matrix (Sec 10).
FR-03a [Should] Session timeout and forced re-auth after inactivity.
                AC: Auto-logout after 30 min idle on financial views; 8 hours on warehouse mobile views.

--- 1.2 Dashboard & Navigation ---
FR-04  [Must]   Landing dashboard with real-time KPIs: Total Products in Stock, Low/Out of Stock,
                Pending Receipts, Pending Deliveries, Scheduled Transfers.
                AC: KPI tiles update within 2 seconds of an underlying transaction, no page refresh.
FR-05  [Must]   Persistent left-hand collapsible sidebar grouped into Products, Operations,
                Analytics, and Settings.
                AC: Sidebar state (collapsed/expanded) persists per user across sessions.
FR-05a [Must]   Role-aware navigation filtering.
                AC: Analytics and Settings entries hidden or read-only for Warehouse Staff role.

--- 1.3 Product Management ---
FR-06  [Must]   Create, update, and archive products (Name, SKU/Code, Category, Unit of Measure,
                Initial Stock, Reorder Threshold).
                AC: Archived products hidden from operational pickers but remain in historical reports.
FR-07  [Must]   Input validation preventing duplicate SKUs, negative stock, empty mandatory fields.
                AC: Duplicate SKU returns a specific, actionable error, not a generic 500.
FR-07a [Should] Bulk product import/export via CSV.
                AC: A 500-row CSV import completes in <=5 seconds with per-row error reporting.
FR-07b [Must]   Per-product supplier and lead-time association.
                AC: Required so MA-02 and MA-06 have the lead-time data they depend on.

--- 1.4 Operations Module ---
FR-08  [Must]   Receipts: input supplier details and received quantities; validation increments
                stock and logs the central ledger.
                AC: Every stock increment traceable to a receipt ID in an immutable ledger table.
FR-09  [Must]   Delivery Orders: picking, packing, and validation steps that decrement stock.
                AC: Partial shipments (short-pick) supported and recorded distinctly from full fulfillment.
FR-10  [Must]   Internal Transfers between locations without altering total inventory count.
                AC: Transfer greater than current location stock is rejected with a clear error.
FR-11  [Must]   Stock Adjustments reconciling recorded vs. physical counts, with mandatory reason.
                AC: Every adjustment requires a reason code from a controlled list
                    (damage, theft, miscount, expiry, other).
FR-12  [Should] Backorder handling for delivery orders exceeding available stock.
                AC: System auto-flags shortfall and links it to the Predictive Reordering Agent's queue.
FR-13  [Should] Batch/lot and expiry-date tracking for perishable or regulated SKUs.
                AC: FEFO (first-expiry-first-out) picking suggestion surfaces automatically during packing.

--- 1.5 Reporting & Analytics ---
FR-14  [Must]   Inventory valuation report (FIFO/weighted-average), scoped to Inventory Manager role.
                AC: Report total reconciles with sum of per-SKU stock x unit cost within rounding tolerance.
FR-15  [Must]   Stock movement history export (CSV/PDF) for any date range and location.
                AC: Export of 90 days of movement history completes in <=10 seconds.
FR-16  [Should] Supplier scorecard view (on-time rate, short-ship rate, avg lead time). Feeds MA-06.
                AC: Scorecard recalculates nightly and on-demand.

--- 1.6 Notifications ---
FR-17  [Must]   In-app and email alerts for low-stock thresholds, pending PO approvals, anomaly flags.
                AC: Alert latency <=60 seconds from triggering event.
FR-18  [Should] Configurable notification preferences per user and per alert type.
                AC: Users can mute a specific alert type without muting all notifications.

--- 1.7 Authentication & Authorization: Screens, Flows & Enforcement (Section 6) ---
FR-19  [Must]   Invite-only account provisioning; no public self-registration.
                AC: No unauthenticated endpoint creates an account without a valid, unexpired invite token.
FR-20  [Must]   Generic, non-enumerating error messaging on login and password-reset request.
                AC: Response time and message content identical whether or not the email exists.
FR-21  [Must]   Access-token / refresh-token rotation with device-scoped "remember me" extension.
                AC: A refresh token used once cannot be replayed; reuse triggers revocation of token family.
FR-22  [Must]   Escalating account lockout after repeated failed logins.
                AC: Lockout thresholds/cooldowns match Section 6.5 exactly; verified via CI brute-force test.
FR-23  [Should] Lightweight re-authentication modal on idle session expiry, preserving in-progress work.
                AC: Re-entering password within 2 minutes of expiry resumes the same screen/state.
FR-24  [Must]   Centralized API-gateway authorization middleware; no per-endpoint ad-hoc role checks.
                AC: Code-review checklist + static-analysis rule fail the build if middleware is bypassed.
FR-25  [Must]   Immutable authentication/authorization audit log, viewable by Admin.
                AC: Log entries cannot be updated or deleted via any application-layer API; append-only.

2. AI MODULES (MASTRA-POWERED) - FUNCTIONAL SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------
MA-01  Smart Inbound Ingestion Workflow
       Trigger: New vendor delivery PDF/emailed packing slip.
       Output: Draft Receipt (Pending Validation); human confirms before posting to ledger.

MA-02  Predictive Reordering Agent
       Trigger: Continuous + event-driven (Delivery Order / Stock Adjustment).
       Output: Draft purchase order queued for managerial sign-off (never auto-executes).

MA-03  Conversational Warehouse Assistant
       Trigger: Staff voice/text command on mobile device.
       Output: Executed transfer (with confirmation above threshold) or spoken/text answer.

MA-04  Inventory Anomaly & Shrinkage Auditor
       Trigger: Nightly batch + rolling 30-day pattern scan.
       Output: Advisory flagged report only; no automated punitive action.

MA-05  Demand Forecasting & Seasonal Trend Agent (New)
       Trigger: Weekly scheduled + on-demand.
       Output: 30/60/90-day demand forecast per SKU feeding MA-02's safety-stock calc.

MA-06  Supplier Performance Scoring Agent (New)
       Trigger: Recalculates after every validated Receipt vs. its PO.
       Output: Live supplier scorecard (on-time %, short-ship %, lead-time variance) -> FR-16.

3. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------
NFR-01 Performance          Page load / API response <=1.5 seconds; UI fluid across
                            desktop, tablet, mobile.
NFR-02 Security             TLS 1.2+ in transit; AES-256 at rest; RBAC restricting financial
                            dashboards; zero cross-role data leaks in pen-test.
NFR-03 Offline Resilience   Local SQLite / service-worker caching; 100% of queued
                            transactions sync without data loss on reconnect.
NFR-04 Code Quality         All imported snippets/external libraries reviewed and tested
                            locally; no unreviewed third-party code merged to main.
NFR-05 Observability (New)  Structured logging/tracing for every Mastra agent decision;
                            100% of agent actions traceable to an audit log entry with
                            reasoning summary.
NFR-06 Data Retention (New) Ledger and audit data retained per compliance policy; PII
                            minimized in agent memory; retention enforced at DB layer.

                     (immutable, append-only)

6. SUCCESS METRICS / KPIs (Section 2 - 90 days post-launch)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Stockout rate                          : reduce by >=40% vs. pre-launch baseline
- Manual receipt data-entry time         : reduce by >=70%
- Mean time to detect shrinkage pattern  : <=7 days from onset
- Forecast error (MAPE), 30-day demand   : <=15% for A-class SKUs
- Warehouse task completion (voice)      : >=25% faster for transfer tasks
- Purchase-order approval turnaround     : <=4 business hours
- System uptime / offline recovery       : >=99.5% uptime / 100% queued-transaction sync

                                    manual Receipt/Delivery tracking (FR-08/09).

                                    responsive layout, Reporting & Analytics (FR-14-16),
                                    Notifications (FR-17-18).

Phase 4 - AI Advanced Agents     : MA-03, MA-05, MA-06.

------------------------------------------------------------------
9. KEY RISKS (Section 15)
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Insufficient historical data for MA-05 on new SKUs -> fallback to category-level averages.
- Voice misrecognition in MA-03 causing incorrect transfer -> confirmation step + audit log.
- Over-reliance on AI purchase drafts -> surface reasoning/confidence score with every draft.
- Stale/mis-entered supplier lead-time data -> MA-06 continuously corrects from receipt data.
- Offline multi-device sync conflicts -> idempotency keys + last-write-wins with conflict log.
